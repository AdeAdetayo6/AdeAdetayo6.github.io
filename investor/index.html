How the funnel works

Every channel points to one page, and the page feeds one list. Each week’s deals go to investors who asked for them, so a deal never starts from zero.

  1. SocialInstagram, Facebook and WhatsApp posts all link to the same page.
  2. This pageOne promise, four fields, only email required. Less to fill in, more sign-ups.
  3. One listA Mailchimp audience with name, email and phone, so contacts can be filtered later.
  4. Weekly emailNew deals across London and the Home Counties, each with the key numbers.
  5. CallReplies turn into calls, and calls into reservations.
Why only email is requiredEvery extra required field costs sign-ups. Name and phone are optional, and keen investors fill them in anyway.
Why one page for every channelOne link to share, one place to change the offer, and one list to measure.
What to measureSign-up rate from each channel, the weekly email’s open and reply rates, and replies that become calls.
